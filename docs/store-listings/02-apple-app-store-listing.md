## Description  _(max 4000 chars)_
SwiftLoan helps you find the right personal loan without the legwork. Instead of applying to lenders one by one, compare offers from multiple RBI-regulated lending partners in one place — then apply with the one that fits.

WHY SWIFTLOAN
• Compare real offers — see loan amounts, interest rates, and EMIs side by side.
• Check eligibility fast — a few basic details, no lengthy paperwork.
• Paperless hand-off — continue your application directly with the lender you choose.
• Built-in loan tracker — follow every application's status in one screen.
• Voice assistant — get help navigating the app hands-free.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us a little about yourself — income, employment, and city.
2. We check your eligibility and show matching offers from our lending partners.
3. Pick an offer and continue securely to the lender to complete your application.

IMPORTANT — SWIFTLOAN IS NOT A LENDER
SwiftLoan is a loan marketplace. We do not lend money, set interest rates, approve applications, or collect repayments. All loans are provided by RBI-regulated lending partners, who are solely responsible for approval, terms, disbursal, and collection. SwiftLoan does not charge you any fee to use the platform.

YOUR PRIVACY
We collect only what's needed to check eligibility and connect you with lenders, and we share your details with a lender only when you choose to proceed. See our Privacy Policy: https://swiftloan.ai/privacy

Questions? Reach us at support@swiftloan.ai
