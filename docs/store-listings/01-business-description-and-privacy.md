## Business description — short (≈250 chars, for Meta "business description")

SwiftLoan is a digital loan marketplace. We help users discover and compare personal-loan offers from RBI-regulated lending partners, check eligibility, and connect with the right lender. SwiftLoan is not a lender and does not disburse loans.

## Business description — long

SwiftLoan is a technology platform that helps individuals in India discover, compare, and apply for personal-loan offers from regulated lending partners (banks and NBFCs). Users share basic profile and eligibility details in the app; SwiftLoan matches them to suitable offers and hands them off to the chosen lender to complete the application.

SwiftLoan acts as a Lending Service Provider / digital sourcing and lead-generation platform. We are not a lender: we do not fund loans, set pricing, approve or reject applications, or collect repayments. All lending decisions, disbursals, and servicing are performed by our regulated partner institutions. We operate in accordance with the RBI Digital Lending Guidelines and applicable data-protection law.

## "Not a lender" disclosure (put this verbatim in-app, on the website footer, and in store listings)

SwiftLoan is a loan-marketplace and does not lend money. Loans are provided solely by RBI-regulated lending partners, who are responsible for approval, interest rates, terms, disbursal, and collection. SwiftLoan does not charge users any fee for using the platform.

### 4. Sharing with lending partners
When you choose to proceed with an offer, we share the details needed to complete your application with the selected RBI-regulated lending partner. That partner then processes your data under its own privacy policy as a data controller. We do not sell your personal data.

### 5. Consent
We collect and share your data only with your explicit, informed consent, given in-app before any sharing. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

### 6. Data security & retention
We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards and retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
