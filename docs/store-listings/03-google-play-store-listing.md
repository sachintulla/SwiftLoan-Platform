## Full description  _(max 4000 chars)_
SwiftLoan is a personal-loan marketplace that helps you find the right loan without applying to lenders one at a time. Compare offers from multiple RBI-regulated lending partners in one place, check your eligibility, and continue your application with the lender you choose.

WHY SWIFTLOAN
• Compare real offers — loan amounts, interest rates, and EMIs side by side.
• Fast eligibility check — a few basic details, no lengthy paperwork.
• Paperless hand-off — continue securely with the lender you pick.
• Loan tracker — follow every application's status in one place.
• Voice assistant — hands-free help navigating the app.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Share a few details — income, employment, and city.
2. We check eligibility and show matching offers from our lending partners.
3. Choose an offer and continue to the lender to finish applying.

IMPORTANT — SWIFTLOAN IS NOT A LENDER
SwiftLoan is a loan marketplace and lead-generation platform. We do not lend money, set interest rates, approve applications, or collect repayments. All loans are provided by RBI-regulated lending partners who are solely responsible for approval, terms, disbursal, and collection. SwiftLoan does not charge users any fee to use the platform.

LOAN TERMS (representative — set by lending partners, not SwiftLoan)
• Minimum & maximum repayment period: set by each lending partner (e.g. 3 to 60 months).
• Interest rates (APR): determined by the lending partner based on your profile (e.g. 10%–36% p.a.).
• Example: for a ₹50,000 loan over 12 months at 20% APR, total payable ≈ ₹55,500 (illustrative only).
Actual terms are shown by the lender before you accept.

YOUR PRIVACY
We collect only what's needed to check eligibility and connect you with lenders, and share your details with a lender only when you choose to proceed. Privacy Policy: https://swiftloan.ai/privacy

Support: support@swiftloan.ai

## ⚠️ Personal Loans declaration  _(Financial features → mandatory)_
Play requires this for any app that facilitates personal loans, **including marketplaces/lead-gen**. Be ready to provide:
- Declaration that the app **provides or facilitates** personal loans (select "facilitates — marketplace/lead generator", not "direct lender").
- **Country of operation:** India.
- **Interest-rate disclosure:** state that rates are set by RBI-regulated partners; include min/max APR range and that max APR does not exceed the regulated cap.
- **Partner details / licence evidence:** names of your RBI-regulated lending partners (and, if asked, their NBFC/bank licence numbers or your LSP agreements).
- **No short-term/high-APR "payday" style products** (Play bans loans with full repayment ≤ 60 days).
- Privacy policy URL that explains data handling for the loan flow.

> Have your legal/compliance owner confirm the exact partner-licence evidence before submitting — this is the section most likely to trigger back-and-forth or rejection.
