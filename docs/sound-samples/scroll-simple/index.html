Simple scroll sounds

Plain, short, normal sounds — pick your favourite. In the app one plays per ~260 px of scrolling, a little quieter than here.

Plain tick: one short neutral tick
Soft tick: a touch lower and rounder
Tiny pop: a smaller version of the click
Light tap: a gentle mid-pitch tap
High tick: crisp but quiet
Low tock: a small, warm knock
Knock: a short wooden-ish knock
Single note: one short soft note
Dot: the smallest, most subtle blip
Quick double tick: two tiny ticks close together
