Scroll sound samples

Tap each one and pick your favourite. They are all at the same gentle level; in the app they play a little quieter and only once per ~260 px of scrolling.

Suup swipe (current): a quick airy swipe that rises
Soft air: a longer, breathier, lower glide
Quick swish: short and light, like a flick
Silk fall: a smooth "shhh" that settles downward
Hush: very gentle, round, almost a breath
Glass slide: a soft tonal glide (no air)
Whisper up: a low, quiet "whooop" rising
Pop roll: three tiny soft pops in a row (like the click)
Picker wheel: two quick soft detent ticks
Paper flick: a short, dry, airy flick
