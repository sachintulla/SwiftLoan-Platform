Scroll roll sounds

No air swipes, no single taps — rolls, ratchets, soft glissandos and tonal slides. Quiet on purpose. In the app one plays per ~260 px of scrolling.

Wheel roll: a few soft detent clicks slowing down, like a mouse wheel
Freewheel: quick ratchet clicks that decelerate and fade
Zip roll: tiny ticks rising in pitch, like a zipper
Dial detents: three low, soft clicks like turning a knob
Harp up: a soft glissando of five notes rising
Harp down: the same glissando falling
Marimba roll: three quick warm notes
Bubble stream: a few tiny pops rising
Slide whistle: a soft pure tone gliding up
Fader: a low, smooth tonal slide, like moving a volume slider
Pluck glide: a plucked string bending down slightly
Pulse roll: soft low pulses, like a gentle rumble rolling by
