Scroll swipe sounds

Real scrolling sounds — smooth swipes, slides and rolls. They are quiet on purpose. In the app one plays per ~260 px of scrolling.

Short swipe: a quick, light "suup"
Medium swipe: a smooth "suuup"
Long glide: a slow, relaxed swipe
Swipe up: the sound rises (scrolling down the page)
Swipe down: the sound falls (scrolling back up)
Finger slide: a dry, glassy slide across a screen
Cloth swish: low and soft, like fabric brushing
Paper slide: airy and light, a sheet sliding
Breeze: a very soft breath of air
Rolling: a smooth roll with a subtle grain, like a wheel turning
Soft roll with body: a low tone gliding under a little air
Light whoosh: the agent's scroll glide, but lighter
