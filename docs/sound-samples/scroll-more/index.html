More scroll sounds

Twelve more plain, short options. In the app one plays per ~260 px of scrolling, a little quieter than here.

Cork pop: a tiny, dry pop
Rubber tap: a soft, slightly muted tap
Tiny bubble: a very small rising blip
Thip: a dry little air tick, no pitch
Tk: a crisp micro-click with a hint of tone
Low drop: a small, round, low note
Soft bell: a gentle, short bell
Click-pop: a tiny click followed by a small pop
Muted tap: a dull, cushioned tap
Gentle beep: one short smooth tone
Felt tick: soft and woolly, like tapping felt
Sparkle: two tiny high notes, light and bright
