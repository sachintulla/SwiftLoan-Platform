## Long version (answers each question one by one)

Hi App Review team,

Thanks for the review. We'd like to clear up the 4.3 and 4.2.6 concerns. Here are answers to
your questions.

**1. Who built the app?**
We built it ourselves. SwiftLoan is made and run in-house by our company, which also owns this
Apple Developer account and the SwiftLoan brand. We did not use any app template or
app-generation service. It is a native app built with React Native, with our own iOS code and
our own backend.

**2. Who owns the brand in the app?**
We do. SwiftLoan is our own product and brand. It is not submitted on behalf of a client or any
other company.

**3. What does the app do?**
SwiftLoan helps people in India compare and apply for personal loans. The user answers a few
basic questions once, we run a soft eligibility check, and we show them real, comparable offers
from regulated lending partners. SwiftLoan is not a lender. The actual loan, interest rate, and
approval come from RBI-regulated lending partners. We are a comparison and referral platform (a
Lending Service Provider).

**4. Who is it for?**
Everyday borrowers in India, including people who are not comfortable with English or with
filling long forms.

**5. What makes it different from other loan apps?**
A few things:
- Voice assistant. The app has a built-in voice assistant ("Ruby") that can guide the user and
  fill the forms by voice, in English, Hindi, and Telugu. This is built for people who find
  typing or English-only forms hard. Most loan apps don't have this.
- One check, many offers. The user enters their details once and we pull comparable offers from
  several different regulated partners through live integrations, so they don't repeat the same
  form in app after app.
- Real side-by-side comparison. We show interest rate, processing fee, GST, total amount
  repayable, and EMI together, and rank by what actually costs the least — not just a headline
  rate.
- Fully multilingual. The whole app works in English, Hindi, and Telugu, not just the marketing
  text.

**6. Is this a duplicate of other apps on your account?**
No. There was one older app with similar functionality on this account in the past. We have
already removed it. SwiftLoan is now the only app of this kind on the account.

**7. Did you use a commercialized template or app-generation service? (4.2.6)**
No. The app is custom-built by our own team.

**8. How can we test it?**
Sign-in uses a mobile number and OTP. We've provided a demo account in the App Review
Information section. All data on that account is test data and no real money moves inside the
app. When the user picks an offer, the app hands off to the lending partner to finish the
application.

**9. Compliance.**
The app follows RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines and India's DPDP Act 2023. We clearly state
that SwiftLoan is not a lender, we ask for consent for each purpose, and we offer in-app
grievance redressal and full in-app account and data deletion. Our privacy policy is live at
https://swiftloan.ai/privacy-policy.

We're happy to provide anything else you need. Thank you for your time.
